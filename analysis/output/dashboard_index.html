How it's measured

Service hours
Actual hours worked minus admin, training and Go-Go hours.
Earned hours
For each open hour, the staff the Sales Factor table calls for given the prep items actually sold (1 person up to 22 items, 2 up to 40, 3 up to 60, then one more per 20), plus the opener and closer allowance. Every open hour earns at least 1 person, so a slow store isn't penalized for its minimum staffing.
Productivity
Earned hours ÷ service hours over the 4 weeks.
SPLH
Toast net sales ÷ service hours.
Slow and busy days
Each store's days split at its own median daily sales.
Mgr forecast vs actual
Manager sales forecast compared with actual sales. Negative means managers forecast low.
